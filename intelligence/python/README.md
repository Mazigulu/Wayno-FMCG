# Wayno Intelligence & Machine Learning (Python)

Data science and ML pipelines driving predictive restock recommendations, localized FMCG price elasticities, and inventory forecasting.

## Core Models
- **FMCG Demand Forecaster**: LightGBM time-series forecasting predicting neighborhood duka flour, cooking oil, sugar, and beverage consumption.
- **Credit Risk & Working Capital Scoring**: Alternative underwriting models scoring retail shops based on historical payment velocity and order cadence.
- **Depot Stockout Early Warning**: Alerting wholesalers 48 hours prior to anticipated stock depletion.
