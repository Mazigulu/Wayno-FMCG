# Wayno Dispatch & Routing Optimization Service

Combinatorial vehicle routing problem (VRP) solver and consignment dispatch optimizer.

## Capabilities
- **Multi-Depot Split Order Minimizer**: Solves SKU distribution across proximate wholesale suppliers to minimize separate delivery fees.
- **Rider Batching & Clustering**: Groups nearby retail deliveries within 15-minute pickup windows.
- **Weight & Volume Packing**: Enforces motorbike 40kg / 60L capacity limits and trike 250kg limits.
